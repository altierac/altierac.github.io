About

Netrunner. Born in Santiago. Grew up on borrowed hardware and stolen bandwidth. Got good enough that Arasaka noticed, which turned out to be the worst thing that could happen to a person.

I write code that breaks things that deserve to be broken. I build systems that keep people free. Sometimes those are the same thing.

If you're reading this, you either know who I am or you don't need to.

  • Cooking when there's time. Chilean food mostly. My mother's sopaipillas recipe lives in an encrypted partition and I'm not sorry about that
