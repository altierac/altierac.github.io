About

Studying history of art at the Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa, with a focus on Italian Renaissance painting and Roman material culture. Currently on exchange at Waseda University in Tokyo, researching the reception of Western classical art in Meiji-era Japan.

Outside of the archive, I work part-time in executive support and project coordination. When neither of those, you'll find me at whatever museum is closest or looking for good coffee.

Interests

  • Classical antiquity and its afterlife in modern culture
  • Comparative aesthetics, East and West
  • Languages (Italian, German, English, French, Japanese)
  • Architecture and urban history
